## Produced outputs/interfaces

`CompanionView({pose:'idle'|'help'|'celebration', visibleGuidance, nextActivityLabel?, onRequestHint, onChooseNext, onRead, onStopReading, audioStatus})` renders approved supplied text and explicit actions. It never reveals another hint body or selects/judges a task; callbacks enter QuestActivity's assistance/selection adapter. Help receipt success determines visibleGuidance. The audio controller owns speaking state and optional voice failure.

`CreativePlot({saved:CreativeState, availableChoices, entitlements, pending, saveStatus, onSave(choice:CreativeChoice), onCancel})` owns only its tentative arrangement/undo stack. Colours/patterns/sockets resolve through WP02-01A's finite catalogue. Use that child's DEC-031 accepted complete arrangement choice to adapt to WP04 `ChooseCosmetic {choice}` in the standard captured profile/token/actionId envelope; one Save is one atomic arrangement. Already-applied means saved without a new celebration, conflict/save-failed keeps a visibly unsaved preview, and cancel restores the latest committed arrangement. Disable stale editing while its Save is pending, and clear the old preview on profile/epoch change. This public prop contract serves the early fixture host, WP02-06A composition and WP02-11A extension; it imports no composer or singleton store.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Pip has idle/help/celebration and approved guidance/activity; uncommitted answer help never revealed.
2. Three scarf/flower colours/three sockets support preview/change/move/undo; explicit Save persists per profile/reload.
3. Failed/conflict action truthful and profile pending-result isolation; 20/60 entitlements never remove free choices/deduct points.
4. Comfortable keyboard/tap/text controls and no-audio/static presentation work.

5. Explicit Save is the only creative persistence boundary: preview, move and undo cannot change points, attempts or saved IDs. Cancel returns to the saved arrangement; failed/conflicting Save keeps an honest unsaved state. Profile switch cannot apply a late save/help result to another child.
6. Demonstrate all three free scarf colours, all three flower choices and three placement sockets, plus locked/unlocked leaf-pattern and planter-rim views. Use committed entitlements, never local score recomputation. The Q3 planter appears only with the committed quest result; ordinary free scarf selection remains available earlier.
