## Produced outputs/interfaces

**Pure signatures.** `classifyRewardEligibility({ track, selection, context }): EligibilityResult` returns eligible-first / eligible-review / resume-existing / practice-only with a stable reason (same-week-used, child-practice, not-due, unfinished-free-practice, familiar-repeat); no IDs/slots are allocated by classification. `applyCheckRewards(input: RewardCheckInput): RewardCheckResult` applies one already-validated new judged Check using cumulative encounter/opportunity counts, not episode-local counts. `applyQuestReward({ profileId, questId, questCompleted, rewards }): { nextRewards, delta }` trusts only WP04's derived static prerequisite/result completion, not a UI flag. `deriveCosmeticEntitlements(lifetimePoints, catalogue)` returns stable choice entitlement IDs; `compactCompletedOpportunity(track, activeWeek)` returns an equivalent compact track without mutating input; `validateRewardState(rewards, competition, profileId, catalogue)` returns invariant issues for WP04's full decoder. These ports use data-only imported DTOs and no state/DOM/audio/network/Date.now.

WP04 supplies the stored pre-Check track and `checkSequence = previous cumulative validChecks + 1`; the pure scoring function advances cumulative facts once, records first Check's week/slot and computes components. Duplicate/out-of-order transport is filtered by WP04; mismatched sequence/facts here are rejected, not counted again. Eligibility at OpenEncounter is provisional until first valid Check. Reclassify against current track/free-practice/success history at that point; an ineligible task still supports ordinary practice/world evidence but cannot mint a reward opportunity. Hints before first Check are retained even when that Check crosses Monday. A null slot after cap remains null permanently: later rollover cannot grant that old opportunity a new slot.

Unsuccessful FinishPractice is not an input event to scoring: WP04 closes a learning episode while reward state is byte-equivalent apart from any independent calendar reconciliation. New episode ordinal or a navigation route never enters the receipt key. For free practice, preserve cumulative validChecks/answerHintUsed and actual-success date/week in the canonical track until success; a later episode does not clear it. On success, update actual lastSuccessWeek/date even when the earning week is older, so the next due-review opportunity waits until a later week than that success. Preserve closed earning-week totals and archives; no current-week slot is consumed by this delayed success.

**Identity and continued reward contract.** Keep three distinct identities: canonical question, reward opportunity and submitted Check. An authored task's canonical ID is stable across appearances; a generated task uses generator family plus canonical mathematical parameters and a deliberately managed reward-equivalence version. Seeds, cosmetic revisions, tile order, alternate valid answers and session IDs do not create a new question. WP03 owns equivalence. A new parameter combination is new only when its validated educational task differs; a release/version bump alone cannot refresh rewards.

A reward opportunity is bound to profile + canonical question + earning week. WP03 may open one for a first encounter, or for due review after a completed earlier opportunity. A review becomes rewardable only in a later competition week than that question's previous success and only when WP03 selects it as educationally due. At most one opportunity for that canonical question can start in a week. An unfinished opportunity continues with its existing attempts/hints; it cannot be discarded to recover first-correct eligibility. Selection and pre-Check hint facts survive reload; first valid Check fixes the earning week and competitive slot. Free practice never opens a reward opportunity merely because the child clicks replay.

This opportunity boundary prevents finite starter content exhausting rewards permanently. Revisit through selected due review is visibly described as review, not a falsely new question. Immediate same-week repeats still give feedback and learning evidence; do not promise points for every repeat or build an endless points farm. A changed week does not automatically make every old task eligible. WP03's review scheduling is the dependency, not a second reward scheduler. Checks and hints in free practice also persist: a task Checked in free practice cannot then become a fresh rewardable first encounter in the same week. Later rewardable review requires completion and a later week than that success, just as for scored practice. Merely switching practice/quest routes never clears attempt or assistance history for an existing opportunity.

**Check, hints and scoring.** Only a valid explicit Check counts. Editing, moving objects, undoing and incomplete input rejected by the response contract are not attempts. Record an answer-relevant Hint before revealing it; an opportunity's hint status cannot be cleared by navigating away. Routine instruction narration does not count as a hint. Passage/order read-aloud changes WP03 reading-support evidence but does not reduce points by itself. Assistance that supplies a method/answer beyond reading the authored words is an explicit Hint. No hidden penalty for an accessibility control.

| Event per reward opportunity | Lifetime points | Competitive points if this opportunity has a slot and its week is open |
|---|---|---|
| First valid Check, right or wrong | +5 once | +5 once |
| Correct on that first Check with no answer Hint | +15 once | +15 once |
| First success after a wrong Check or answer Hint | +5 once instead of the +15 component | +5 once instead of the +15 component |
| Further Checks, duplicate delivery or replay of this opportunity | 0 | 0 |

Totals are 20 for unassisted first success, 10 for supported/later success, and 5 until success. First-correct and later/supported-success awards are mutually exclusive. No time-based bonus, penalty or tiebreak exists. A new weekly due-review opportunity begins its own explicit attempts, with prior learning history retained. Receipts are per profile/opportunity/component; a submission ID separately makes transport retries idempotent.

**Cap and fair selection.** The first 30 WP03-selected eligible opportunities actually Checked per profile per week reserve the 30 competitive slots, including eligible due review. Every valid first Check consumes one slot even if wrong. Hints/retries use the same slot; reload, abandon, changing task or profile rename does not release it. Slot eligibility is decided from recorded selection before Check, not retroactively chosen from best results. Child-selected easier/free-practice tasks are noncompetitive and do not create fresh reward opportunities. New selected work and eligible due review after the cap still receive lifetime awards and world/learning progress, with a clear “weekly points complete; adventure progress continues” message.

The first-30 cap limits playtime advantage; it does not make adaptive difficulties psychometrically equal. Do not multiply by difficulty, label local rank as ability, invent common assessments, or add cap-management administration. A child can keep practising with no timer or daily lockout. The cap is fixed product policy, not a child/adult tuning control.

**World rewards.** Each of the three starter quests grants +20 lifetime points once per stable quest ID, never competitive points. Expansion grants the same once-only +20 for each of its ten total quests, including the retained starter quests. Replays, rewards shown again after reload and revisiting completed world locations grant no quest bonus. Do not add special-challenge +30 scoring in this package: no distinct required challenge justifies a second bonus category; mark this part of PROP-023 explicitly bounded out. No extra competitive awards based on quest access or amount of unlocked content.

Accepted starter cosmetic entitlements at lifetime 20 (companion accessory choice) and 60 (village decoration choice) are both reachable even with supported answers during the three quests. Expansion adds 150 (decorative building appearance) and 300 (another decoration/companion choice), using WP02's finite creative catalogue. Thresholds unlock permanent choices, do not spend/deduct lifetime points and do not lock educational quests. WP02 binds the thresholds to concrete assets without changing score mathematics. Missing presentation cannot revoke earned entitlement. Medal presentation grants no additional points.

Apply DEC-021 concrete bindings through WP02 catalogue: 20 leaf scarf pattern, 60 planter rim, 150 facade trim, 300 star/wave scarf patterns; initial scarf colours/Q3 planter remain free. Reuse explicit original policy, not a third-party game economy: no current package code exists and generic economy templates do not fit the accepted identity/cap/review semantics.

CanonicalRewardTrack and compaction invariants follow WP05-01A: preserve full unfinished opportunities, free-practice assistance/attempts and current-week slot facts; latest completion remains retry-idempotent, older exact contributions fold into closedAwardTotal/completedThroughOrdinal. Old ordinals/removed encounters never reopen. WP04 owns submission/tombstone integration; WP03 owns evidence windows.
