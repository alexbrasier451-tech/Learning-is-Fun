## Produced outputs/interfaces

Use the exact ReconciliationInput/Result, CompetitionProfile, PersonalRecords and ClosedWeekResult types from WP05-01A. `rankPositiveScores(entries)` returns positive entries with competition rank and prospective medal category; it does not award. `closeWeek({ competition, profiles })` returns the closed result (or none for zero participation) and updated personal-record map, with no reset outside its returned values. `reconcileCompetitionWeek(input, nowEpochMs)` composes closure and opening. `buildLeaderboardReadModel({ competition, profiles, context })` produces the read-only Hall view; current ranks are provisional and medal counts come only from closed records. `removeProfileResults({ competition, personalRecordsByProfile }, profileId)` removes current score/slot entries and historical identity entries, sets omission flags, deletes that profile's personal records, and leaves surviving ranks/medals/bests unchanged. `validateCompetitionState(competition, profiles)` returns date/rank/slot/aggregate invariant issues for WP04. No function imports SaveDataV1 or obtains time/storage itself.

Dates and ranks use WP05-01A helpers/types exclusively. Stable display order uses nickname then immutable ID only after rank assignment. Current-week scores are sums of slot contributions; closed archives snapshot positive results and nickname/avatar at closure. An empty active week advances latestOpenedWeek without a result; an archive emptied by profile deletion remains an omission-labelled historical week and is subject to the same 52-week retention. Personal records remain independent of archive removal. Return a new personal-record value only for genuinely changed records, but never mutate supplied maps.

**Calendar and closure.** One stored installation timezone, Europe/London, applies to every profile and is shown in adult help; no timezone setting in this package. A week is calendar Monday 00:00 to next Monday 00:00 in that zone, including daylight-saving changes. A linked app opened abroad retains that zone. Backups preserve it; different device/browser zones do not reinterpret saved weeks. No trusted server clock or anti-cheat service.

On startup, foreground resume, opening standings, or before any state-changing action, reconcile the calendar. At a later week close the last active week, persist its result/medal entitlements exactly once, and open the actual current week. A foreground boundary refreshes displayed standings without requiring a background timer. Skipped inactive weeks create no fake participants/champions. Sunday remains provisional. Any later festival celebrates the latest closed result at the next visit, never finalises an unfinished Sunday week.

A retry success after its opportunity's earning week closes gives only the remaining lifetime component; it cannot revise that archive or use a slot in the current week. No new opportunity for this canonical task can open until a later week than that success and WP03 considers review due. All rollover plus triggering-action consequences use WP04's atomic commit. Latest-opened-week state prevents automatic reopening on clock rollback. During rollback keep the latest week active and show an adult clock notice; do not apply negative or duplicate resets. A forward jump may close a week early; say that local clock changes/old-backup restore can alter this local history rather than claim tamper resistance.

**Standings/history.** Rank by competitive total only; equal positive totals share competition rank (1, 1, 3) and corresponding gold/silver/bronze (gold, gold, bronze). A stable display order may use nickname then ID but cannot break the sporting tie. Zero-score profiles appear as “not yet scored”, unranked and unmedalled. Show current local week, points, used slots out of 30 and lifetime total separately. The board identifies the browser-local scope and gives a no-player/one-player state without synthetic opponents.

Archive the most recent 52 completed weeks with nonzero participation, preserving dates, timezone, policy version, scores, ranks and historical nickname/avatar snapshots; label this retention limit in history. Keep all-time personal weekly best and cumulative medal counts plus necessary once-only receipts independently of this display window. When a record is tied, do not manufacture a new higher best. WP04 deletion removes that profile's linked entries/personal records without reranking survivors or reissuing medals. Ordinary rename leaves historical snapshots unchanged. An old-backup import is an atomic complete replacement, never merged scores or receipts; it can rewind history and cannot be described as secure anti-cheat.

**Calendar implementation.** Consume WP05-01A's completed localDateAt/weekKeyFor/addCalendarDays and injected CompetitionClock; do not duplicate its Intl/date arithmetic. WP04 samples one instant outside the reducer and supplies it throughout this operation. No implicit host timezone or elapsed seven-day arithmetic enters closure/review.

Implement reconcileCompetitionWeek using the exact ReconciliationInput/Result signature owned in WP05-01A and accepted by DEC-030; do not redeclare its output shape here. Initial null latestOpenedWeek opens the observed week with no archive. Compare ordered date keys: forward movement closes only the previously active participating week, updates personal records once, clears current scores/slots and opens the actual observed week. No empty intervening archives. Earlier observed weeks retain the latest active week and return a notice. Same-week input produces no closure delta. Pass the context plus both returned state surfaces to WP04's single commit; replaying the resulting state gives no second medal/best/archive change. Later success's actual active week governs next-review eligibility, not its old earning week.

At startup, foreground resume, standings entry and before mutations WP04 dispatches reconciliation. While visible, WP01 requests a clock read at most once per minute; reconcile only when the local date/week changes. It requires no background timer reliability and does not promise a UTC midnight timestamp API. Browser timezone-rule data is the local dependency; an unsupported explicit zone reports a clock/storage recovery state rather than silently switching to another zone. No date library, trusted server/time request or temporal polyfill is required.

Keep calendar/standings pure and injected-clock based. ReconcileCompetitionWeek uses the completed early date helpers; temporal plan invokes pure closeWeek and publishes nextCompetition/context/closedWeekChanges. Native utilities and date-library exclusion are authoritative in [WP05-01A](WP05-01A.md). No remote leaderboard or time authority. Stable display order does not break competition ties.
