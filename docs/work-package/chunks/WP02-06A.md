Onboarding is embedded in a safe first manipulation and explains Check, undo/remove and hints. The map begins around the village green and shows the three reachable quest destinations; future regions are scenery or clearly described future destinations, not dead controls. The first slice includes four finished locations: Village Green, River Bridge, Whispering Library and Market Square.
