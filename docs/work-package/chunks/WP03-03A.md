## Exact scope

Anchor text has fixed words/case/comma and only terminal marks chosen from ., ? and !:
- “Where are my magic books?” is required for the first line.
- “Look, there they are.” and “Look, there they are!” are equally accepted for the second: calm observation or excited discovery. A question mark on that second line is outside the supplied discovery context.
Both slots must contain one allowed mark before Check. Blank slots are incomplete; malformed marks/slot maps invalid; complete wrong combinations judged. Both lines are a single canonical scored question; different slot feedback does not create surprise extra opportunities.

Author 11 further tasks with explicit statement/question/discovery contexts. Changing names alone does not create meaningful distinct work. Every task enumerates all legitimate answers available through the controls, accepted/rejected examples, explanation, two non-answer-revealing hints and separate assisted worked support. Treat punctuation as meaning in that context, not a universal terminal-rule or arbitrary free-text grader. Do not strip assessed punctuation/spelling, match substrings or normalize an error into correctness.

Conservatively label introductory anchors/same-demand practice support. Only genuine authored objective/demand justifies core; do not invent stretch. Every record supplies exact curriculum objective/section, demand rationale, review disposition/provenance and prompt/instruction/assessed-text roles.

Neutral instructions/control read-aloud stay available. Before Check, speech cannot name the missing mark or model the completed sentence in a way that reveals it. Reading assessed text is listening-supported evidence where relevant, separate from answer hints; help spoken aloud retains its text's answer-assistance meaning. Music/effects give no answer-only clue. Silence all/Stop/music ducking runtime belongs to WP02 under DEC-020; WP03 supplies safe content roles and expected semantics.

## Acceptance criteria

Exactly 12 reviewed canonical tasks including the compound anchor; every constrained answer combination is accepted/rejected/incomplete/invalid for an explicit reason, no unresolved ambiguity. Both anchor second endings receive equal credit, first line wrong punctuation fails; blank/malformed responses are unscored. Anchor alternate endings/slots/reorder/week do not refresh identity. Every record has reviewed examples/explanation/two hints/worked support and source/demand rationale; narration cannot reveal missing punctuation before Check.

For the anchor's nine complete terminal-mark pairs, exactly two are correct and seven are judged wrong with contextual feedback; missing/unknown/extra slots are dispositioned separately. The review records the text actually offered for neutral narration, each hint and worked support, including when each may be revealed. A music/effect toggle or unavailable local voice changes none of this answer matrix, and text remains sufficient to finish the activity.
