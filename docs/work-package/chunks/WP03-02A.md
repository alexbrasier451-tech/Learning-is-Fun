## Exact scope

Bridge anchor: choose one to six reusable whole-metre planks of lengths 1–6; sum exactly 12 m. Accept every valid arrangement including 6+6, 4+4+4 and 1+2+3+6; order irrelevant. Empty is incomplete; permitted nonempty wrong sum is judged. Out-of-range lengths/count/type are invalid-response, not wrong arithmetic. Return under/over feedback and explain how a plank change changes total without privileging an arrangement or auto-correcting.

Bridge pool: targets 6–17 inclusive, same supply and six-plank maximum. Deterministically enumerate 12 meaningful target identities. Provide an independently derivable reachable solution for every target; target 12 remains the anchor regardless of arrangement.

Merchant anchor instruction: “Please pack twice as many apples as pears. I need nine pieces of fruit altogether.” Accept exactly apples=6, pears=3 with both total=9 and apples=2×pears. Complete response uses nonnegative integers within total≤24; distinguish blank fields/empty basket (incomplete), malformed/out-of-bounds (invalid) and complete wrong counts (judged). Zero for one fruit with a nonempty allowed basket is a real wrong answer, not a missing input.

Merchant pool: multiplier 2 or 3, pears 1–6, total=(multiplier+1)×pears. Enumerate all 12 unique pairs, including (2,3); every solution uses at most 24 fruit. Distinguish correct total/wrong relation, correct relation/wrong total and both wrong without inferring a conceptual diagnosis from one basket.

Each record needs primary assessed objective, curriculum reference, genuine demand rationale, reviewed explanation/two hints and worked support. Hint 1 redirects attention; hint 2 gives a strategy/representation without the answer; worked example/solution is separately assisted. Neutral controls/read-aloud are not scoring hints. Audio/feedback cannot be sole correctness cue.

Treat fixed anchors and unchanged-demand domains conservatively as introductory support; larger target/multiplier alone does not establish core/stretch. Core requires actual justified objective/response demand within accepted controls. No invented harder band. Each 12-item pool exceeds the three-distinct-task readiness requirement but does not promise 30 unseen suitable weekly tasks.

## Acceptance criteria

All 24 tasks solve within exact controls/bounds and reproduce from saved parameters. At least three materially different bridge anchor constructions pass; permitted wrong totals produce under/over judgement. Merchant (6,3)/(5,4)/(4,2)/(2,2) yield correct/relationship-only wrong/total-only wrong/both wrong. Drafts are never attempts; blank/malformed/content-error tags remain unscored. Every task has source/band rationale, two hints, worked support and honest M04 mixed evidence. Alternate arrangement/order/cosmetic changes preserve canonical ID.

The boundary matrix also distinguishes six legal planks from seven invalid planks, a legal nonempty over-target bridge from malformed lengths, a 24-fruit valid-domain response from a 25-fruit invalid response, and zero of one fruit from a wholly empty basket. Each of the 24 records carries an independently checked reachable answer, a complete wrong answer and its expected feedback category. No task is accepted merely because its generator and evaluator agree with each other.
