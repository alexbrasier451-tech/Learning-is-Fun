# Human-directed music replacement

9 October 2026. Controller-owned execution amendment, within the ongoing implementation stage.

The first source audition received the human response “Needs changes; I’ll describe them”, followed by: “Sounds pretty bad :-) rip the music off from free sources online instead unless you can do better with what you have?”

The Controller chose the requested existing-free-music route. WP02-03A's original-only music/composition fallback is superseded by this direct human instruction. Select and locally bundle recordings with explicit licences permitting game use and redistribution, retain creator attribution, original source and licence evidence, and record reproducible conversion, loop and gain changes. No paid service, account or unlicensed recording is authorized. Source recordings plus transformation metadata replace original note scores as the provenance/reproduction input for the two themes.

The village and quieter library roles, two themes, five effects, roughly 60–90-second musical loops where suitable, runtime catalogue, local/offline assets, measured metadata, audio controls and actual listening obligations persist. Do not damage an arrangement by arbitrary trimming to match a duration; report a necessary bounded refinement to Controller. The human criticism specifically names music; the existing five effects may remain subject to mix and listening review.

The rejected synthesized exports are not accepted for delivery. Their old technical report is historical evidence only. The original audio author retains sole audio-source/runtime/catalogue/register ownership for replacement; the independent media reviewer will recheck the changed source/licence/technical boundary. Real listening and human feedback remain necessary; numeric measurements never establish perceived musical quality. Downstream game work continues independently.
