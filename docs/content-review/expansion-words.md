## Narration and help disposition

The neutral instruction names only the control action and Check, never a missing answer, correct option or spelling. It is safe for every task and does not set answer-help eligibility. E01 blocks assessed-context/word speech before Check: affix pronunciation, stressed forms, spelling letters and reading alternatives could reveal the assessed derivation or spelling. The conservative false flag is deliberate; it is not a demand to penalise ordinary control narration. No hidden answer is inserted at a blank.

E02 and E03 permit assessed-text listening support. E02 narration reads the visible context with a pause at each blank, never filling it or expanding/spelling a chosen option. Homophone choices remain visibly differentiated; ordinary pronunciation cannot identify their spelling. E03 narration reads the visible context without selecting, glossing, emphasising or substituting an answer. These read-aloud uses are listening-supported evidence, not answer hints and not a points reduction. Reading an explanation, spelling out the answer, completing a blank or selecting/emphasising the right option is explicit answer help, not ordinary narration. These policies require the receiving UI/audio owner to implement them; no audio was produced or heard here.

Every task below has separately authored attention and strategy hints which avoid naming its accepted choice, plus answer-revealing worked support. Hint use sets answerHintUsed; worked support sets workedSupportUsed, both sticky downstream. These change supported-success/help eligibility, independently of the reading/listening evidence mode. Pure evaluation consumes no help, reward or durable-state fields.

- Primary: E01 / E01-spelling-affixes; **support**.
- Source: Year 1 consolidation; Year 1 — Writing: transcription — prefix un-; consolidated for Years 3–4; English Appendix 1, Year 1, prefix un- (p. 6).
- Demand: Earlier un- prefix consolidation: a concrete action and its reversal are supplied; recognise one transparent word.
- Context: The book strap is fastened. Choose the word meaning to undo the fastening.
- Narration: neutral instruction only before Check; assessed spelling/affix pronunciation withheld.

| ID | Offered alternative | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| a | refasten | Rejected |
| b | unfasten | Accepted |
| c | fastening | Rejected |

Lexical rationale: Unfasten means undo the fastening. Refasten means fasten again; fastening names the action or attachment, not its reversal.
