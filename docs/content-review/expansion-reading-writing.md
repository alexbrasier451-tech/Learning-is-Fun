## Source, identity and review method

Official reference checked 11 October 2026: [DfE English programmes of study](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-curriculum-in-england-english-programmes-of-study/national-curriculum-in-england-english-programmes-of-study). Source codes used below:

| Code | Official programme location and scoped use |
|---|---|
| R34 | Years 3–4, Reading — comprehension: retrieval of non-fiction information. These notices are original fictional non-fiction forms. |
| I34 | Years 3–4, Reading — comprehension: infer, justify with evidence, predict from stated/implied details. |
| I56 | Years 5–6, Reading — comprehension: supported inference and reasoned justification, with greater text/viewpoint complexity. |
| W34 | Years 3–4, Writing — composition: constructing narratives and evaluating/editing consistency and clarity. |
| W56 | Years 5–6, Writing — composition: evaluating changes for meaning/effect and cohesion. |

Support revisits introductory demand within the scoped programme. Core is Year 4-centred within the official two-year band. Stretch means the particular extra reasoning below; only I56 and W56 items claim selected Years 5–6 demand. Neither a word limit nor a longer string alone defines attainment. The programme covers far more than these activities. E10 is a finite construction/editing substitute; it cannot establish extended-writing proficiency.

All records have family `english.reading-writing`, equivalence `r1`, empty generated parameters and the immutable authored key shown at each heading. The key identifies the **particular supplied passage plus assessed question**, not just a template. Canonical IDs are generated by the frozen `canonicalQuestionId` utility and exported in `READING_WRITING_MANIFEST.taskIds`; no identity depends on answers, option/tile presentation order, help, retry, skin, seed, release or week. `contentRevision: 1` is separate. Substantively changing an item requires a reviewed equivalence decision; the validator rejects changed source, meaning, answers, metadata or help under a frozen identity. No M1 task or binding is replaced. Repeated bindings and alternative valid answers never add to the count.

Each item has its exact source section, band rationale, two separate strategy hints and explicit worked support in the task record. The matrix entries below are post-Check review evidence, never source alternatives or pre-Check hints. Text-only `stimulus: null` means there is no hidden diagram or source alternative containing a computed answer. The fixed controls include all complete alternatives; selected answers are semantic IDs. Choice/clue sets have at most four options; sequences have three to five distinct sentences; source passages are at most 120 words (sequence context plus all tiles also fits 120).

Inventory: E08 = 3 support / 4 core / 3 stretch; E09 = 3 / 4 / 3; E10 = 3 / 4 / 3. All thirty have different texts and meaning, not renamed people or shuffled clones. E08 links location, set membership, cross-reference, condition, prerequisite, exception, content-to-container, amended instructions, archive request and time/condition. E09 distinguishes likely cause, physical state, immediate prediction, emotion, prior activity, motive, growth pattern, limits of measurement, implied expectation and limits of a trace. E10 covers referent/temporal dependencies, parallel events, ownership clarity, tense, alternate noun descriptions, uncertainty and contrast.

## E09: all inference × clue combinations

Every matrix cell is a complete jointly assessed answer. `A` = accepted pair; `I` = unsupported inference; `C` = the inference is supported by the passage but this clue does not establish it. Rows and columns are exact option IDs. The accompanying explanations disposition every I/C cell. An omitted inference or clue is incomplete regardless of whether the present part is good. Each complete response yields exactly one E09 result, one Check and one ordinary answer/success opportunity, never a clue skill or reward. Predictions and interpretations are qualified, not certain diagnoses of characters.

## E10: complete construction/edit answer spaces

Sequence disposition uses independently authored before-relations in the test oracle; production stores explicit full accepted arrays. Every complete permutation is checked: 6 + 6 + 24 + 24 + 120 = **180** orders. An order is valid exactly when all listed relations hold. Every other order is rejected for one or more named relation violations. This rule exhaustively dispositions the finite space without dumping 169 wrong permutations into the review. All ordered proper subsets are incomplete even if not yet extendible in that order; a child can still rearrange before Check. Unknown or duplicated tiles are invalid, never assessed mistakes.

## Evidence, narration and access review

Every item separates `instructionText`/`narration.neutralText` from `assessedText`; sequence tiles and all answer-relevant textual content must be treated as assessed material when read aloud. Normal narration of the question/control instructions has no assistance or attempt effect. Reading a source passage aloud is allowed before Check. For this family, downstream adapters should carry `assessedTextReadAloud: true` and `evidenceMode: listening-supported`, preserving correctness, access, ordinary points and quest progress. A successful listening-supported reading episode is not independent reading evidence. Neither silence/volume controls nor instruction replay may create attempts or clear help.

H1 and H2 for every item were reviewed above as non-answer strategy prompts. They remain explicit hint assistance under the frozen help contract; the UI records the hint before displaying/speaking it. Worked support and post-Check explanations can reveal answers and must never be substituted into neutral narration or an accessible source alternative. Worked-support/hint flags stay sticky through navigation, retry, reload, revisits and release. Different response formats or accepted clues do not create separate rewards.

The pure tests pass a **constructed consumer observation** through the accepted learning reducer and a constructed reward opportunity through the accepted scorer: neutral first success gives independent evidence; read-aloud gives supported evidence with the same ordinary 20 first-success points in that eligible fixture; hint/worked help gives supported evidence and the ordinary 10 supported-success points. These are scoped policy fixtures, not a promise of points in every history/cap/week situation. E09 produces one primary skill, one completed episode and two ordinary receipt components (answer plus one success), never an extra clue component. Sticky help is also checked across a wrong Check then a successful continuation.

The frozen M1 state adapter retains its existing evidenceMode when recording assessed narration, which was appropriate to its punctuation task. Therefore **WP04-07A must integrate the objective-specific reading/listening qualification** when this family becomes live. This producer does not edit state, central catalogue/dispatch/bindings or UI. No save/reload or published audio result is claimed here.
